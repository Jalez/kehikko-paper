Kind: feature

- **Other modules find the paper without naming it.** Modules that point at the paper, or suggest a change to it, now find it as "the document that takes suggestions", so they keep working with Paper as it is and with anything that takes its place.

## For agents

- Paper now offers the `document.proposals` service: its folder (`.kehikot/paper/{epic}`), its main file and its tools `list_sections`, `read_source`, `propose_edit` and `list_proposals`. A module that uses Paper finds it with `services.find` and calls the tools the descriptor names.

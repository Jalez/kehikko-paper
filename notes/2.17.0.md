- **One summary for an agent's suggestions.** When you have answered every suggestion an agent made on a file, and decided nothing more for half a minute, that agent hears once what you accepted and declined, with your reasons, instead of a message after each decision.
- **A reason added later still reaches it.** Writing a reason beside a suggestion you already declined puts it in that summary, or sends a short one of its own if the summary has already gone.

For agents: you get one summary when the person has answered all your proposals, not one message per decision.

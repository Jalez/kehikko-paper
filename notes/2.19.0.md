- **Suggestions no longer go missing from the paper.** The paper could show no suggestion waiting while some were, until it was reloaded. It now always catches up with what is waiting.
- **Accepting a change no longer declines others in your name.** Accepting a suggestion sets aside only the suggestions about the same words, and says they were set aside by your change; the rest stay waiting.
- **Decline asks why, right where you press it.** The first press on Decline opens a short field beside the change; write a line and press Enter, or press Enter or Decline again to decline without one.
- **Clearer list of what was not accepted.** Each entry says whether you declined it, it was set aside by a change you accepted, or the agent withdrew it, with its file, section, the passage and a Show button to go back.
- **Suggestions on a second build's files.** A second main file in the paper folder, such as the one a university template builds the submission from, and the files it loads, can now be suggested about and reviewed like the rest of the paper.

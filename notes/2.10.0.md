- **Select text in the PDF.** Drag over the compiled PDF to select exactly the words you want, across lines, columns and pages, and copy them. A selection also points at that passage for the canvas, so notes, suggestions and agents get it, just as a press on one word does.
- **Links in the PDF work.** Clicking a web link in the paper opens it in the Browser on your kehikko when there is one, and in a new tab when there isn't. Links within the paper, such as references and the table of contents, scroll to their place.
- **Presses still work as before.** A single press still selects the word in the source and a double press still opens the source. A drag no longer counts as a press.

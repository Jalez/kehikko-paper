- **Your paper, source and PDF side by side.** Paper shows the LaTeX of the epic's paper in an editor and, beside it, the PDF a real LaTeX engine compiles it to, so what you see is what you will submit.
- **Save and the PDF follows.** When you pause typing the file is saved and compiled, and the new PDF replaces the old one in place.
- **Jump between source and PDF.** A selection in the source shows where it came out in the PDF, and pressing the PDF goes to the line it came from.
- **Errors you can press.** A failed compile shows each error with its file and line, and the last good PDF stays on screen.
- **Review an agent's suggestions.** A change an agent suggests is drawn in the source where it sits, for you to accept or reject; only the picked parts of the epic are shown.

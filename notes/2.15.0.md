- **Say why you decline a suggestion.** An agent's suggested change now has Decline with reason… beside Cancel change: write a line, press Enter, and the change is declined with your reason. Cancel change is still one press, and an empty reason declines without one.
- **Add or change the reason afterwards.** Changes you declined stay listed over the source for an hour, where you can add, edit or clear the reason.
- **For agents:** a declined suggestion carries the person's reason in list_proposals, and the agent session that suggested it is told "Declined: …" with the reason when it can be reached.

- **Quiet when you are not looking.** Paper no longer checks for changes while its container is folded, while you are on another kehikko, or while its window is hidden. When you come back it catches up at once.
- **Changes arrive as they happen.** A suggestion from an agent, an edit in another editor, an agent writing the file directly, or a git checkout shows up in Paper within a moment, instead of on the next check a few seconds later.
- **Lighter on your machine.** Keeping up with a paper now takes one small request when something changed, instead of two requests every four seconds.

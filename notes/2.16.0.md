- **Search the paper with ⌘F.** Press ⌘F over the PDF to open Kehikot's find bar. It searches every page of the paper, not only the pages on screen. Matches are highlighted and counted ("3 of 17"), and the PDF scrolls to the current one. Enter and ⇧Enter move between matches, and Escape closes the bar.
- **The source keeps its own search.** ⌘F in the LaTeX source still opens the editor's own search, and Kehikot's bar leaves the source alone.
- **No more "Opening…" flash.** Kehikot's own loading cover stays up until the paper is drawn, then lifts on the finished page.
- **The compile log stays as you left it.** Its toggle is kept per container and comes back when you return to the kehikko, beside the pane, file and scroll positions Paper already kept.
- **The accepted count moved to the footer.** "3 accepted" now sits in the bottom row with the other readouts, and the header's Accepted changes menu just lists them.
- **A failing page says why.** If the page ever fails to draw, Kehikot shows the reason instead of a blank container.
